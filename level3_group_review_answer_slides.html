Part A · Vocabulary

Questions 1–7

1. Daily exercise is _______ for your health.

Correct answer
beneficial

2. Accountants must be _______ to avoid mistakes with numbers.

Correct answer
detail-oriented

3. She is taking a _______ class to learn how to repair cars.

Correct answer
vocational

4. He used a map to _______ through the new neighborhood.

Correct answer
navigate

5. Moving to a new city was a big _______ for my family.

Correct answer
transition

6. The apartment building needs regular _______, like fixing lights.

Correct answer
maintenance

7. My assistant needs to _______ more interviews for this week.

Correct answer
set up
Part B · Multiple choice

Questions 8–12

8. I forgot to drink water all day, _______ I felt very thirsty.

Correct answer
so
cause → result (same pattern as Q15)

9. If she takes classes, she _______ a better job.

Correct answer
will get
first conditional

10. He is _______ student in the class.

Correct answer
the fastest
superlative

11. I _______ go to bed late when I was in college.

Correct answer
used to
past habit (not am used to)

12. I decided _______ early in the morning.

Correct answer
to jog
decide + to-infinitive
Part B · Multiple choice

Questions 13–17

13. finished in: He finished the project quickly.

Correct answer
Verb

14. quickly modifies the verb.

Correct answer
Adverb

15. She was thirsty, _______ she drank water.

Correct answer
so
not because (reverses cause)

16. The manager _______ the new employee last week.

Correct answer
trained
simple past + finished time

17. She has been studying English _______ she moved to Boston.

Correct answer
since
Point in time — not for or during
Part B · Best sentence

Questions 18–21

18. Circle the correct sentence.

Correct answer
He has gone to the career center twice.
Not has went — irregular past participle

19. Circle the correct sentence.

Correct answer
I have lived here for three years.
for + length · not since three years

20. Circle the correct sentence.

Correct answer
He doesn't have enough experience yet.

21. Circle the correct sentence.

Correct answer
If I get the job, I will celebrate.
If-clause = present, not will get
Present Perfect

Questions 22–26

22. She _______ (eat) sushi before.

Correct answer
has eaten

23. Derek _______ (forget) his password many times.

Correct answer
has forgotten

24. I _______ (not write) the report yet.

Correct answer
haven't written / have not written

25. They _______ (see) that movie three times.

Correct answer
have seen

26. We _______ (be) to New York twice.

Correct answer
have been
Tip: Q26 — be → been (not beed or was).
Present Perfect · Continuous

Questions 27–32

27. He _______ (take) the career workshop twice.

Correct answer
has taken

28. She _______ already _______ (send) her résumé.

Correct answer
has … sent

29. I _______ never _______ (go) to a vocational school.

Correct answer
have … gone

30. How long _______ you _______ (speak) English?

Correct answer
have … been speaking

31. She _______ (drive) to the clinic since January.

Correct answer
has been driving

32. They _______ (run) a small business for five years.

Correct answer
have been running
Tip: Q30–32 — PPC = has/have been + -ing; since = point in time, for = length.
Part C · Unscramble

Questions 33–36

33. Unscramble: used · I · to · drive · to · work

Correct answer
I used to drive to work.

34. Unscramble: never · she · has · gone · to · vocational · school

Correct answer
She has never gone to vocational school.

35. Unscramble: If · I · get · the · job · I · will · celebrate

Correct answer
If I get the job, I will celebrate.
Comma optional after job

36. Unscramble: has · already · taken · He · the · test · twice

Correct answer
He has already taken the test twice.
Part D · Writing samples

Questions 37–41

37. If + present, will + verb — career or education

Correct answer
If I get a better job, I will take more classes.

38. Present Perfect + irregular participle + for/since

Correct answer
I have lived in Boston for two years.
Also accept other correct PP + for/since

39. Superlative — compare two people or things

Correct answer
Maria is the most detail-oriented student in our class.

40. One sentence with used to + one in the present

Correct answer
I used to work on Saturdays. / I work full-time now.

41. Use two words from the Part A word bank

Correct answer
It is beneficial to navigate a career transition with vocational training.
Any two word-bank words used correctly

Accept other correct sentences if grammar matches the prompt.

Wrap-up

Common mistakes to discuss

  • so vs because — Q8 & Q15: first clause = cause, second = result → so
  • used to vs am used to — past habit vs current comfort
  • for vs since — length vs starting point
  • has went → has gone
  • If + will in the if-clause → If I get…, I will…
  • PPC — don't forget been: has been studying
