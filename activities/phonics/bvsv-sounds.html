Pronunciation Practice: B vs V Sounds
In Spanish, B and V usually sound the same. In English they are different.
B = /b/ (both lips together)
V = /v/ (top teeth on bottom lip, voice on)

Reading: "Building a Better Business"

Bella and Victor grew up in the same village but came from very different backgrounds. Bella's family owned a small bakery, while Victor helped his parents sell vegetables in the market. Even though their families worked in different businesses, both Bella and Victor believed that a good business should serve people with care. They often talked about opening a store together one day.

Before starting anything, they decided to visit various shops in nearby towns. They observed how each business treated customers, how products were displayed, and how workers behaved. Bella carried a small notebook and wrote down what she noticed. Victor took photos of beautiful store layouts. They wanted to learn what made a business successful and valuable to the community.

After several weeks of research, they created a plan. They wanted a place that was easy to find and visible to many people, so they chose a small space between the village market and the bus station. The location was busy, but the building was old and needed a lot of work. Still, they believed they could transform it into a beautiful store.

Every afternoon after their regular jobs, Bella and Victor cleaned, painted, and repaired the space. They organized the shelves, labeled the products, and made sure everything looked neat. They invited family and friends to visit the store before it opened so they could give honest feedback. Bella and Victor valued every suggestion and made changes to improve the store.

On the first day, the store offered various useful items, including books, bags, kitchen tools, and fresh vegetables from local farms. Customers loved how bright and organized the store looked. Many people visited simply because they had heard good things from neighbors. Bella and Victor treated every customer with respect, and people felt comfortable asking questions and giving ideas.

Within a few months, their business became very successful. Other shop owners visited to learn from their work. Bella and Victor believed the real value of their business came from kindness, hard work, and listening to people. They continued improving the store, adding new products, and welcoming feedback. The village was proud of their success, and Bella and Victor were proud to serve a place they loved.

Comprehension Questions

2. Where did they open their store?

3. What kinds of things did they sell?
