Grading Criteria

Score each section individually to identify specific problem areas.

Mastery (0-1 Errors) Student can reliably distinguish these sounds. Move to next pair.
Developing (2-3 Errors) Student is inconsistent. Needs focused practice with listening drills before speaking.
Needs Focus (4-5 Errors) Student cannot hear the difference. High Priority. Start with exaggeration and visual cues (mouth position).
