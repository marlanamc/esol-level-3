Teacher's Answer Key - Week 4

Use this guide to conduct Activity 1 (Listening) and check student answers for Activities 2 and 3.

Activity 1: Listening - Minimal Pairs

Read the bolded word clearly for each pair. Do not repeat unless necessary.

1. The Waiter serves food at the restaurant.
2. I work in a big Warehouse with many boxes.
3. The Web designer creates beautiful websites.
4. I get my Wages (money) every Friday.
5. William starts his new job on Wednesday.
