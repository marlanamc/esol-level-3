Sentence Writing Reference Sheet

Use this reference sheet to help you write sentences. Choose words from each word bank to create complete sentences.

Sentence Building Framework
WHO
→
VERB
→
WHAT
→
WHERE
→
WHEN
→
TIME

Ask yourself: WHO is doing it? VERB - what action? WHAT are they doing it to? WHERE? WHEN? TIME EXPRESSION?

Word Banks

I you he she we they my friend my sister my brother my mom my dad my coworker my teacher my classmate my neighbor
VERBS
eat drink cook clean study read watch listen walk call go make wash take do
WHAT (Objects)

WHERE (Places)

Sentence Formulas by Tense

Present Simple
Formula
WHO + VERB (V1/V1-3rd) + WHAT + TIME
Affirmative: I eat breakfast every day.
Negative: I don't eat breakfast every day.
Question: Do you eat breakfast every day?
Present Continuous
Formula
WHO + am/is/are + VERB-ing + WHAT + TIME
Affirmative: She is cooking dinner right now.
Negative: She isn't cooking dinner right now.
Question: Are you cooking dinner right now?
Past Simple
Formula
WHO + VERB (V2) + WHAT + TIME
Affirmative: They watched the news yesterday.
Negative: They didn't watch the news yesterday.
Question: Did you watch the news yesterday?
Past Continuous
Formula
WHO + was/were + VERB-ing + WHAT + TIME
Affirmative: He was studying at home last night.
Negative: He wasn't studying at home last night.
Question: Were you studying at home last night?
Future Simple
Formula
WHO + will + VERB (V1) + WHAT + TIME
Affirmative: I will call my friend tomorrow.
Negative: I won't call my friend tomorrow.
Question: Will you call your friend tomorrow?
Future Continuous
Formula
WHO + will + be + VERB-ing + WHAT + TIME
Affirmative: We will be cooking dinner tonight.
Negative: We won't be cooking dinner tonight.
Question: Will you be cooking dinner tonight?
