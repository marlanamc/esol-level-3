Assessment Guide

How to Run It

  • Start casual: "Hi! How are you?"
  • Ask the questions: (Use the clean prompt sheet)
  • Let it flow: If they're doing well, ask simple follow-ups like "What did you study?" or "Did you like it?"
  • If they get stuck: Repeat or rephrase. "Take your time," "You're good!"
  • Note: We only need their first name. No corrections needed during the chat—just focus on understanding.

Scoring Rubric

⭐ 3 – Strong Understands easily, answers clearly, sounds confident.
⭐ 2 – Getting there Understands most, answers with some pauses or small mistakes.
⭐ 1 – Needs support Trouble understanding or answering, unsure or hesitant.
Quick Scoring Thought:
• Easy/Natural → 3
• Little effort for both? → 2
• Pretty difficult? → 1
