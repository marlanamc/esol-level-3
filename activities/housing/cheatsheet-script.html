Housing Scripts - Quick Reference Cheat Sheet

Common Sentence Patterns

  • My address is [your address].
  • I live at [address].
  • There is a [problem/emergency].
  • There is a [problem] at [location].
  • I saw a [thing].
  • I see [thing] in my [place].
  • I smell [thing] in my [place].
  • I think someone is [condition].
  • A [person] is [condition].
  • It is not [working/okay].
  • It is very [adjective].
  • I cannot [verb].
  • I will [verb] on [date/time].
  • I am [verb-ing] [when/where].
  • Please [verb] [what].
  • Please send [help type].
  • Can you [verb]? Thank you.
  • What should I do?

Sentence Starters

Hello. This is [your name].
Example: Hello. This is Maria.
Hello. I live at [address].
Example: Hello. I live at 45 Green Street.
I need [help/thing].
Example: I need help. I need an ambulance.
There is a [problem].
Example: There is a fire. There is a problem.
There is a problem with [thing].
Example: There is a problem with the heat.
Please help. Thank you.
Example: Please help. Thank you.
Can you [verb]?
Example: Can you help? Can you fix it?
I have a [question/problem].
Example: I have a question. I have a problem.

Emergency Words

Emergencies: fire smoke car accident a person hurt gas smell problem
Help Needed: help ambulance fire department police ambulance and fire ambulance and police
When (Urgent): now immediately right now as soon as possible

Housing Problem Words

Problems: no heat no water broken not working leak water coming from noise problem very loud
Things That Break: heat water stove light refrigerator door window elevator
Where Problems Are: ceiling wall floor kitchen bathroom apartment
Conditions: worse getting worse very cold very hot very loud

People and Conditions

People: child person cook worker someone
Conditions (Injured/Hurt): hurt injured not moving cannot move
Ages: 3 years old 5 years old 7 years old 10 years old

Time Words

When It Happened: just now 10 minutes ago 20 minutes ago 30 minutes ago 1 hour ago
Time of Day: night morning afternoon evening today tomorrow
Days of Week: Monday Tuesday Wednesday Thursday Friday Saturday Sunday
Dates (Examples): March 10 April 5 May 1 June 15 next week

Location Words

Places: Maverick Station outside my building on Main Street in my apartment in my home in my kitchen in my bathroom
Directions: outside inside at from to

Action Words

What You Do: sleep study work leave go wait pay clean fix
What You See/Smell: see saw smell hear
What You Need: need want have think

Questions:
