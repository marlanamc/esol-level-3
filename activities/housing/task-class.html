🏠 Housing Task Class

Four real-life housing tasks, arranged easiest to hardest. Each one is a complete, realistic task on its own — not a grammar lesson. Sentence starters and phrase banks are there when you need them, and there's less of that help in each later task as your skills grow.

Why it's built this way: Every task here is a real, whole thing you'd actually do — not a worksheet about grammar rules. Help (sentence starters, word lists) shows up right when a task needs it and fades out as the tasks get harder, so you build up to handling real conversations without support. If you want extra practice with a specific verb tense or sentence pattern along the way, that's available separately and is optional, not a prerequisite.
