Unit 4: Consumer Smarts Taboo

Describe the word to your team — without saying the three words below it. 16 words from this unit.

How to play

  1. One student describes the big word to their team.
  2. They may not say any of the three crossed-out words — or the word itself.
  3. The team guesses. 60 seconds per turn.
  4. Press Got it for a point, or Skip to pass.
