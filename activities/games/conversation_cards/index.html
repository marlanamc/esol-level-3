ESOL Level 3

Conversation Cards

Ten printable decks, one per unit. Every deck has 24 cards in four categories — discussion, role-play phone calls, and scenarios. Draw a card, flip it, then talk.

Open a deck and press Draw. One card at a time, big enough to project, with no repeats until the deck runs out. Tap a category to draw only phone calls, only scenarios, and so on. Space flips the card, → draws the next one.

Teachers: the link at the top right of any deck switches to the printable sheets — landscape, two-sided, flipped on the long edge. 8 sheets, 6 cards each. Cut on the lines and each question lands on the back of its own category.
