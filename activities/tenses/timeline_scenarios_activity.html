Timeline Scenarios

Use timelines to understand real stories about study, work, and moving. First solve the situation, then use past, present, and future language when it helps.

🕰️ Scenario 1

Solve Maya’s Story And Build The Timeline

Put Maya’s life events in order on the timeline. Then answer questions that show you understand her story, not just the dates.

Clues About Maya

  1. After four years of study, Maya finished her nursing degree in May 2021.
  2. She moved from Bogota to Miami in January 2022.
  3. In Miami, she worked as a prep cook at a Cuban restaurant.
  4. She moved to Boston in August 2024.
  5. In December 2024, she started working as a line cook at an Italian restaurant.
  6. She has been working as a line cook since 2024.
  7. By 2027, she will have been living in the U.S. for 5 years.

Use The Clues

Questions

  1. What had Maya already finished before she moved to the U.S.?
  2. Why do you think Maya first worked as a prep cook in Miami?
  3. What changed after she moved to Boston?
  4. If Maya goes to a job interview in 2027, what will she be able to say about her time in the U.S.?
