🕰️ Scenario 1

Solve Maya’s Story And Build The Timeline

Put Maya’s life events in order on the timeline. Then answer questions that show you understand her story, not just the dates.

Clues About Maya

  1. After four years of study, Maya finished her nursing degree in May 2021.
  2. She moved from Bogota to Miami in January 2022.
  3. In Miami, she worked as a prep cook at a Cuban restaurant.
  4. After she moved to Boston in August 2022, she took English courses for two years.
  5. In September 2024, she started working as a medical assistant.
  6. She has been working as a medical assistant since 2024.
  7. By 2027, she will have been living in the U.S. for 5 years.

Questions

  1. What had Maya already finished before she moved to the U.S.?
  2. Why do you think Maya worked as a prep cook first, and then later became a medical assistant?
  3. What do Maya’s two years of English courses suggest about her goals in Boston?
  4. If Maya talks about her life in 2027, what will she be able to say about the progress she has made?
