⭐ Building a Timeline of Where You Have Lived

The same timeline can be described in different ways.

Basic Facts
I lived in El Salvador. (Simple Past)
I moved to Chelsea in 2022. (Simple Past)
I moved to Revere in 2024. (Simple Past)
Now I live in Revere. (Simple Present)
More Detailed Past
I finished high school before I moved to Chelsea. (Simple Past)
I had just finished high school when I moved to Chelsea. (Past Perfect)
I lived in Chelsea from 2022 to 2024. (Simple Past)
I had lived in Chelsea before I moved to Revere. (Past Perfect)
Present And Future Connection
I have lived in Revere since 2024. (Present Perfect)
I have been living in Revere for 2 years. (Pres. Perfect Cont.)
In 2027, I will live in the U.S. (Simple Future)
By 2027, I will have been living in the U.S. for 5 years. (Future Perfect Cont.)
Try this: Start with a simple sentence like "I moved to Revere in 2024." Then make it more detailed: "I have lived in Revere since 2024."

🧠 Remember

You are not choosing the right tense.
You are choosing your focus.
