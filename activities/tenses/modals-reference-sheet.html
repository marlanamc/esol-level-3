🗣️ Opinions & Modals Reference Sheet

💬 Say Your Opinion

  • In my opinion, …
  • I think …
  • I believe …
  • For me, …
  • I feel like …
In my opinion, people should help others.

⚖️ SHOULD / SHOULDN’T

Advice / opinions

  • People should + verb
  • People shouldn’t + verb
People should exercise.
Students shouldn’t use phones in class.

📋 HAVE TO / DON’T HAVE TO

Rules / reality

  • I have to + verb
  • I don’t have to + verb
I have to work today.
I don’t have to wake up early.

🚨 MUST / MUSTN’T

Strong rules

  • You must + verb
  • You mustn’t + verb
You must follow the rules.
You mustn’t smoke here.

📌 REQUIRED

When something is necessary

  • It is required to + verb
  • You are required to + verb
You are required to show ID.
It is required to wear a seatbelt.

🚪 ALLOWED TO

Permission

  • be allowed to + verb
  • not be allowed to + verb
I am allowed to use my phone.
We are not allowed to eat in class.

🔁 Add a Reason

  • because …
  • so …

💬 Keep Talking

  • What do you think?
  • Do you agree?
  • Why?

🧠 Model Answer (from the game)

Question: Must people pay for public transportation in your country?

In my opinion, people should pay for public transportation because it helps support the system. However, in my country, some people don’t have to pay. For example, children and older adults are sometimes allowed to ride for free.
