Get Ready To Compare Your Country And The U.S.

Next week, you will work in groups of 3 with a volunteer. Practice these useful phrases now so you feel more ready to compare life in your country and life in the United States.

1. In my country..., but in the U.S....

Use this to compare two places.

Model: In my country, buses are crowded, but in the U.S., buses are usually quieter.
Your sentence:

2. In my country, people usually..., but here, people usually...

Use this to talk about habits or daily life.

Model: In my country, people usually eat dinner late, but here, people usually eat earlier.
Your sentence:

3. One big difference is that...

Use this to introduce an important comparison.

Model: One big difference is that rent is much more expensive in U.S. cities.
Your sentence:

4. One thing that is similar is that...

Use this to talk about something the same.

Model: One thing that is similar is that families enjoy spending weekends together.
Your sentence:

5. I prefer..., because...

Use this to share your opinion and give a reason.

Model: I prefer taking the train, because it is faster and less stressful.
