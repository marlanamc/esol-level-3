# Verb Bootcamp — Get Ready for the Game

**Goal:** Know the six patterns by heart so you can form sentences quickly when you see the cards.

---

## 1. The Six Meanings → One Pattern Each

| **Meaning** (card) | **Tense** | **Pattern** | **Example (she + walk)** |
|--------------------|-----------|-------------|---------------------------|
| **habit** | Present simple | subject + **V1** (he/she/it: V1**-s**) | She walks. |
| **finished** | Past simple | subject + **V2** | She walked. |
| **now** | Present continuous | subject + **am/is/are** + **V1-ing** | She is walking. |
| **background** | Past continuous | subject + **was/were** + **V1-ing** | She was walking. |
| **future** | Future (will) | subject + **will** + **V1** | She will walk. |
| **before now** | Present perfect | subject + **have/has** + **V3** | She has walked. |

- **V1** = base form (walk, eat, cook)  
- **V2** = past simple (walked, ate, cooked)  
- **V3** = past participle (walked, eaten, cooked)

---

## 2. Say It in Your Head (Affirmative)

For each meaning, use: **subject + pattern.**

- **habit** → *I walk. / She walks.* (add -s for he/she/it)
- **finished** → *I walked. / She walked.*
- **now** → *I am walking. / She is walking.*
- **background** → *I was walking. / They were walking.*
- **future** → *I will walk. / She will walk.*
- **before now** → *I have walked. / She has walked.*

---

## 3. Negative & Question (Same Helper, Different Order)

| **Tense** | **Negative** | **Question** |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Present simple | subject + **don’t/doesn’t** + V1 | **Do/Does** + subject + V1? |
| Past simple | subject + **didn’t** + V1 | **Did** + subject + V1? |
| Present continuous | subject + **am/is/are not** + V1-ing | **Am/Is/Are** + subject + V1-ing? |
| Past continuous | subject + **was/were not** + V1-ing | **Was/Were** + subject + V1-ing? |
| Future (will) | subject + **won’t** + V1 | **Will** + subject + V1? |
| Present perfect | subject + **have/has not** + V3 | **Have/Has** + subject + V3? |

---

## 4. Must-Know: am/is/are and have/has

| **Subject** | **Present (be)** | **Past (be)** | **have/has** |
|-------------|------------------|---------------|--------------|
| I | am | was | have |
| you | are | were | have |
| he / she / it | is | was | **has** |
| we / they | are | were | have |

---

## 5. Instant Practice (Do in Your Head or Out Loud)

Pick **subject + V1** (e.g. *they, cook*). Say one sentence for each meaning.

1. **habit** → *They cook.* (or *He cooks.*)
2. **finished** → *They cooked.*
3. **now** → *They are cooking.*
4. **background** → *They were cooking.*
5. **future** → *They will cook.*
6. **before now** → *They have cooked.*

Try again with: **she + eat**, then **we + study**.

---

## 6. Common Irregulars (V2 and V3 for the Game)

| V1 | V2 (past) | V3 (past participle) |
|----|-----------|----------------------|
| go | went | gone |
| have | had | had |
| do | did | done |
| eat | ate | eaten |
| see | saw | seen |
| make | made | made |
| take | took | taken |
| come | came | come |
| get | got | got/gotten |
| write | wrote | written |
| read | read | read |
| drive | drove | driven |

---

**You’re ready.** In the game you’ll see: **Meaning + Time word + Subject + V1 + Sentence type.**  
Use the pattern for that meaning, then add the time word and make it affirmative, negative, or a question.
