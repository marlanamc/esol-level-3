🔎 Timeline Mystery: Who Took The Tip Jar?

A tip jar disappeared from the back counter at Lago Bistro on Friday night. Your team is the detective squad. Read the clues, rule people out, and find the most likely suspect without overthinking.

👥 Teams Of 3 📖 Reader 🕒 Time Detective 🧩 Evidence Finder 🎤 Speaker
🔍 Part 1 — The Case

Case File

The tip jar disappeared at about 9:15 p.m. on Friday from the back service counter at Lago Bistro. Only two employees were connected to that area. Your first job is simple: figure out who can be ruled out and who still looks suspicious.

📋 Known Facts

  • The jar was still there at 8:55 p.m.
  • It was gone when the manager checked at 9:15 p.m.
  • The back counter is near the kitchen door and the staff lockers.
  • No customers were allowed in that area.
  • The security camera near the back counter was broken that night.
  • One employee reported hearing a locker door close around 9:12 p.m.

🧩 Clues

  • A dishwasher says one suspect had already left the kitchen before 9:00.
  • A camera in the dining room shows one suspect was carrying bread trays at 9:10.
  • One suspect had been waiting near the staff lockers before the jar disappeared.
  • The manager remembers one suspect saying, "I'll be back here in a minute," around 9:05.
  • A server saw one suspect walking quickly from the back hallway toward the parking lot at 9:14.

📚 Key Vocabulary

alibi — proof that a person was somewhere else when a crime happened
suspect — a person police think may have done something wrong
evidence — facts or information that show what really happened
rule out — to decide someone probably did not do it
doesn’t match — when a story and the facts are different
witness — a person who saw or heard something happen

🎮 How To Play

  • Round 1: Read the case and circle the suspect who already looks safest.
  • Round 2: Read each alibi and choose only the 2 most important time clues.
  • Round 3: Compare suspects, read the new evidence, and make one final team decision.
  • Tip: You only need the strongest clues, not every detail.
  • You do not need every detail. Look for what matters around 9:15 p.m.
