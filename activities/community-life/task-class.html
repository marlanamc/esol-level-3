Take Part in Civic Life
Understand a ballot question, share your opinion at a public comment period, or speak up for something your community needs. The hardest task: opinions, persuasion, and a more formal tone — with the least help.

Why it's built this way: Every task here is a real, whole thing you'd actually do in Boston — not a worksheet about grammar rules. Help (sentence starters, word lists) shows up right when a task needs it and fades out as the tasks get harder, so you build up to handling real conversations without support. If you want extra practice with a specific verb tense or sentence pattern along the way, that's available separately and is optional, not a prerequisite.
