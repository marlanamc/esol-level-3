  • Create your own class survey with one question for each quantifier.
  • Walk around and ask different classmates your questions.

  • Try to speak to a new classmate for each question.

Grammar Reference: Countable vs. Uncountable

COUNTABLE Nouns

Things you can count (1, 2, 3...)

Use:

  • many (How many hours?)
  • few (I have few friends.)
  • fewer (fewer jobs than before)

Examples: jobs, classes, bills, rooms, bedrooms, roommates, apartments, neighbors, leases

UNCOUNTABLE Nouns

Things you cannot count

Use:

  • much (How much time?)
  • little (I have little money.)
  • less (less stress than before)

Examples: time, money, rent, space, noise, heat, furniture, storage, privacy

Note: Use most and least with BOTH countable and uncountable nouns.

Example

Question: How much time do you spend on homework every night?

Answer: Nadia spends two hours on her homework every night.

Housing ideas: rent, roommates, rooms, noise, space, neighbors, heat

Word Bank (Optional Help)
